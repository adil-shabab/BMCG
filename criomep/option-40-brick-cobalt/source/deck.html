Construction

Built on a blueprint

Module

16 × 8 brick

Every brick shares one proportion, with 2-unit mortar joints.

Bond

Half-brick offset

Every second course shifts by half a brick — a true running bond.

Mask

C between R17 & R46

Only bricks that fall inside the C-ring are laid.

Corners

1.6 radius

Softened edges keep it friendly at small sizes.
